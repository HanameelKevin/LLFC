# Design Guidelines - Lifeline Family Church

## Visual Style

- **Palette**: Warm, earthy, and welcoming. Avoid sterile clinical tones. Use deep blues, soft golds, and warm neutrals.
- **Typography**: Editorial, high-contrast, clean sans-serif for readability.
- **Elevation**: Minimalist, soft shadows.
- **Layout**: Spacious, asymmetric, editorial-focused.
