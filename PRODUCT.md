# Lifeline Family Church - Product Identity

## Overview

Lifeline Family Church (Nanyuki, Kenya) is a Christ-centered community. Pastor Kevin Murungi leads this spiritual home where "Love is not Love until you give it away."

## Brand Personality

- Warm, inviting, authentic, and restorative.
- "You don't have to be perfect to come here."

## Strategic Principles

- Focus on broken/searching individuals.
- Community and belonging.
- Practical truth & transformative gospel.

## Users

- Broken, weary, or seeking individuals.
- Families looking for community.
- Youth searching for purpose.
