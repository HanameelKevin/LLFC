# Shape Brief: Plan Your Visit

## Objective

A dedicated "Plan Your Visit" section that feels like a warm invitation.

## Core Features

1.  **Warm Welcome**: Hero-style header with a compassionate message.
2.  **Mission & Identity**: Concise section articulating the church's heart.
3.  **Service Schedule**: Clear, readable table or grid of prayer/service times.
4.  **Directions**: Big, clear "Get Directions" button that links to Google Maps.
5.  **Contact**: Immediate access to WhatsApp, phone, email.

## Design

- Warm, inviting palette (deep navy, soft golds).
- Editorial typography.
- Spacious layout, no cards if possible (let content breathe).
