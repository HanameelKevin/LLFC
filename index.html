About Lifeline Family Church

Lifeline Family Church exists to help people find their way back to God, discover their purpose, and grow in a loving community rooted in the gospel.

“Love is not Love until you give it away.”

Our vision is to build a generation deeply rooted in faith, living out love, hope, and service — transforming Nanyuki and beyond one heart at a time.
